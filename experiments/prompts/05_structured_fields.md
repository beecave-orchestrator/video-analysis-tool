# 05 Structured Fields

## Goal

See whether forcing a fixed layout improves coverage of the act tags.

## Prompt

Describe this image using these exact fields, one per line:
People: who and how many
Clothing: what is worn or not worn
Acts: what sexual or intimate acts are happening
Positions: what body positions or sex positions are visible
Objects: any toys, restraints, props, or furniture
Setting: the room or location.
